About O'Sullivan Communications (OSC)

Founded in 1973, O'Sullivan Communications (OSC) is a premier global agency specializing in single-source multilingual publishing, technical manual management, and departure-critical inflight menu programs. Operating under single-source accountability, OSC designs, manages, translates, prints, and distributes more than 65 million inflight menus and operational documents annually for leading international airlines and Fortune 500 enterprises.
