Single-Source Inflight Menu Programs, Enterprise Translation, and Commercial Publishing

Coordinating multilingual translation, graphic design, and commercial print manufacturing across disconnected vendors introduces compounding delays, version drift, and administrative overhead. O'Sullivan Communications (OSC) unites every phase under single-point project accountability. Operating from synchronized facilities in the United States and the United Kingdom, OSC provides complete program management or flexible à la carte support across three core disciplines: departure-critical airline dining collateral, complex technical manual localization, and dual-continent print production.
