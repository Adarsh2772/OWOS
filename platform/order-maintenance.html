Client / Project [Global Travel Brand — Illustrative Project]
Sector Cross-Border Airline & Hospitality
01

Automated Schedule Ingestion

Ingests flight schedules in any format, applies cabin seat counts, load factor buffers, and station routing logic, and flags schedule anomalies against prior rotations to eliminate under-catering and overprinting.

02

Station & Route Governance

Authoritative operational databases maintain airline catering kitchens, shipping stations, aircraft configurations, and service-class template rules—assigning lot identifiers and consolidating menus sharing common food or language elements.

03

Imposition & Cost Tracking

Automatically calculates multi-up press forms, packing manifests, and station shipping labels while tracking cycle revisions and delivered print volumes for transparent client budgeting.
